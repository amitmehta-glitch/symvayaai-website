Door 1 of 5

Builders

Data engineers, data scientists, ML/LLM and MLOps engineers, solution architects and developers.

What to prepare

  • Your CV or LinkedIn profile
  • GitHub, a portfolio or links to work you've shipped
  • Availability: hours per week and earliest start
  • Your expected rate band

How full registration works

  1. Choose the Builder door and sign up with your email or phone.
  2. Read the privacy notice and choose your consents.
  3. Upload your CV or link GitHub and build your skills profile.
  4. Add your role, stack, clouds, industries, availability and rate band.
  5. Tell us what you want to do next: build, lead, or switch into AI.

What happens next

Register your interest now. From 15 December 2026 we invite cohorts in order, and these steps begin.

  1. When invitedYou complete your full profile
  2. Within 48 hoursProfile review and your verification plan
  3. Within 2 weeksA screening interview for your role, with free practice first
  4. ThenProof of Build: a small real task, reviewed by Founding Fellows
  5. You're inBuilder badge, guild access, and matching to squads opens

New to AI? You'll start with a free assessment and an Academy bridge course, then an apprentice role on a squad.

Guardians

Regulation, risk and compliance experts who make sure AI is safe and lawful to deploy.

What to prepare

  • Your CV or LinkedIn profile
  • Regulations and frameworks you know (DPDP, RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, EU AI Act, NIST, ISO 42001)
  • Sectors you've audited or advised
  • Relevant certifications, if you have them

How full registration works

  1. Choose the Guardian door and sign up.
  2. Read the privacy notice and choose your consents.
  3. Upload your CV and list the regulations and sectors you know.
  4. Add availability, rate band and preferred engagement type.

What happens next

Register your interest now. From 15 December 2026 we invite cohorts in order, and these steps begin.

  1. When invitedYou complete your full profile
  2. Within 48 hoursProfile review by the Governance Guild
  3. Within 2 weeksCase review: walk a senior Guardian through how you'd assess a sample AI use case
  4. ThenShadow review on a live regulated project
  5. You're inGuardian badge, the Governance Guild, and compliance roles on squads

Every regulated squad includes a Guardian, so verified Guardians see steady work across many clients.

Companies

Enterprises, GCCs, mid-market firms and startups that need AI in production.

What to prepare

  • A work email
  • The problem you want solved, or a package you're interested in
  • Industry, timeline and budget range
  • Any regulations that apply

How full registration works

  1. Tell us who you are, using your work email.
  2. Pick a package, or describe your problem by industry and use case.
  3. Ask for an NDA first if you need one. Please keep confidential details out of the form until it's signed.

What happens next

  4. At kick-offYou fund escrow and the squad starts
  5. Every weekA one-page status update until sign-off
