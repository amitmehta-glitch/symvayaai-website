Questions people ask

Who should answer?
Anyone who can speak for how AI work is chosen, built or run: business unit heads, the CIO or CTO, data, risk and compliance, operations and finance leaders.
Does it cost anything?
No. The assessment is free.
Should I answer for my best project?
No — answer for what is true for most AI work in your organisation, or for the business unit you name.
Can I stop and come back?
Yes. Answers save in your browser as you go. To finish on another device, use “Continue on another device” at the bottom of any page: it gives you a link that carries your answers.

Your data

121connects is responsible for your data. We collect your name, work email, role, seniority, organisation, its industry, size and type, and your answers. We use them to prepare your organisation’s readiness report and, if you ask, to email you a copy of your answers. During testing, your data is stored with our hosting provider on servers outside India.

Before you send your answers, you choose, separately, whether your answers may be used anonymised and combined with other organisations for the India benchmark, whether your organisation may be named as a participant, and whether we may contact you about the results. All three are optional and none changes your report. Names, emails and answers by organisation are never published.

To see, correct or delete your data, write to rupesh.sreedharan@121connects.in.
