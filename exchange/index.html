How it works

Every door has its own steps. See exactly what to prepare and what happens after you register.

For professionals

  1. Join your door and build your full profile in about 10 minutes when your cohort opens.
  2. Get verified with a skills test and a Proof of Build task.
  3. Get matched to squads on your skills, industry and availability.
  4. Ship and grow, from Verified to Certified to Squad Lead.

For companies

  1. Pick a package or describe a problem by industry and use case.
  2. Scope it with us on a call within 48 hours.
  3. Get a proposal with a certified Squad Lead, milestones and a fixed price.
  4. Ship to production, with milestones paid through escrow.

Questions

When does the Exchange open?

The Exchange launches on 15 December 2026. Founding Fellows and Founding Clients join first, then we invite everyone on the waitlist in cohorts, in the order they joined.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Professionals join free. Companies pay for the packages and squads they hire.

I'm not in AI yet. Can I join?

Yes. Career switchers start with a free assessment and an Academy bridge course, then an apprentice role on a squad.

How are squads chosen?

We check skills, availability and verification level, rank on fit and track record, and a SymVaya lead approves every squad.

Who owns the work?

The client. SymVaya signs one contract with the client, and every member signs an IP assignment.

How is my data protected?

We follow India's DPDP Act. Right now we collect only what's on the form, for the reason we state, and no company sees it. When the platform launches, member data will be hosted in India, you'll give separate consent for each use, and no company sees your profile unless you opt in. Read the privacy notice.
