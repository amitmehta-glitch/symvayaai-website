Why AI stalls in Indian enterprises, and how we get it moving.

India uses AI more than almost anyone. Few enterprises can prove it pays.

Across the major studies published this year, AI use is close to universal and proven value is rare. We call that distance the adoption–value gap. Closing it is the work we do.

75% of Indian leaders say AI has not yet returned enough to justify its cost, against 65% across all markets surveyed.
Avanade, AI Reality Report 2026

  • 97%of Indian managers and leaders use AI regularly. BCG
  • 47%of enterprises have several GenAI use cases live in production. EY–CII
  • 40%report significant or full AI use, against 28% globally. Deloitte
  • 17%are reinventing core processes or business models with AI. Deloitte
  • 12%report significant, measurable returns from AI. ET Edge CIO&Leader
  • 11%run AI agents in autonomous workflows. ServiceNow

Blue bars show how widely AI is used; gold bars show how much of it is changing the business. Each figure comes from a different study, sample and definition, so read the pattern rather than a single funnel.
