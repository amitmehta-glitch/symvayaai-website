Privacy notice for the SymVaya Exchange waitlist and registrations

Last updated 26 November 2026. This notice explains, in plain language, what we collect through the Exchange waitlist and registration forms, why, and how you control it. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025.

1. Who we are

SymVaya AI ("we", "us") runs SymVaya Exchange. We decide why and how your personal data is used, so under the DPDP Act we are the data fiduciary. You can reach us at amitmehta@symvayaai.com.

2. What we collect

Only what you type into our forms:

  • Waitlist form: your name, email, the door you're joining through, and your city if you give it.
  • Registration form: the same details plus, if you choose to give them, your mobile number, role, years of experience, skills, a LinkedIn, GitHub or portfolio link, what you want to do next and your availability.
  • Companies and solution providers: your organisation's name, website and industry, your job title, and a short description of your need or product.
  • Your choices: whether you consented, whether you want the newsletter, and the date and time you submitted.

We don't ask for your CV, ID documents, bank details or any sensitive personal data at this stage. Please don't put them in the free-text boxes.

3. Why we use it

  • To review your registration, place you in a cohort and invite you when the Exchange launches on 15 December 2026.
  • To contact you about your registration, a Founding Fellow or Founding Client application, or your company's brief.
  • To send the Exchange newsletter, only if you ticked that box.
  • To understand, in total numbers only, who is joining (for example, how many Builders in each city), so we can plan cohorts.

We don't use your details for anything else, we don't sell them, and no company or member of the Exchange sees them.

4. Who handles it for us

Our website and forms are hosted by Netlify, Inc., which stores form submissions on its servers, which may be outside India. Our email provider handles the emails we send you. Both act only on our instructions. When the Exchange platform launches, we will move member data to servers in India and delete it from the form service.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your details until you join the Exchange platform (at which point you'll see a new notice and give fresh consent), or for 12 months after you submit them, whichever comes first. We then delete them. We delete them sooner if you ask.

6. Your rights and how to use them

Email amitmehta@symvayaai.com from the address you registered with to:

  • Withdraw your consent at any time. It's as easy as giving it: one email. We'll stop using your details and delete them. Withdrawing doesn't affect what we did before you withdrew.
  • See a summary of the details we hold about you.
  • Correct, complete or update your details.
  • Have your details deleted.
  • Name someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to.
  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter, using the link in any newsletter or by email.

We'll reply within 7 days and act on your request within 30 days.

7. Grievance officer

If you have a concern about how we've handled your data, contact our grievance officer:

Amit Mehta, Founder
amitmehta@symvayaai.com

We'll acknowledge your complaint within 7 days and aim to resolve it within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Keeping it safe

Only the SymVaya team members who review registrations can see submissions, and their accounts are protected with strong passwords and two-step sign-in. If a breach affected your data, we would tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

9. Children

The Exchange is for professionals and companies. Please don't register if you are under 18.

10. Language and changes

You can ask for this notice in any language listed in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution of India, including Hindi. If we change this notice, we'll update the date at the top, and if the change affects how we use your details, we'll email you first.
